FORMAÇÃO & HISTÓRIA

A BANDA PERSEVERA

Conheça os músicos, a trajetória e a proposta autêntica de Heavy Metal da Persevera.

Luiz Carlos • Danilo Bredda • Claudio Lima • Thadeu Castro • Luiz Fernando • Leandro Pieroni

MANIFESTO & HISTORY

THE MIGHT OF PURPOSE-DRIVEN METAL

Formed in Sumaré, state of São Paulo, Brazil, PERSEVERA was born with the mission to revive the raw grit and melodic nobility of classic Heavy Metal, fusing the timeless drive of the '80s and '90s with modern Groove Metal aggression.

Following the widespread acclaim of their conceptual debut album Genesis (2023), the band expanded its lineup to the iconic three-guitar attack — a legendary setup consecrated by heavy metal royalty such as Iron Maiden and Helloween — delivering rich three-part harmonies, massive rhythm foundations and soaring intertwined guitar leads both in the studio and live on stage.

2020 Foundation Year
3 Frontline Guitars
13+ Original Released Tracks
SP Sumaré / São Paulo, Brazil
OFFICIAL BIOGRAPHY & JOURNEY

THE BAND'S HISTORY

From 25 songs forged in isolation on loop pedals to the thunderous live impact of three guitars.

"I wanted a name that wasn't a metal cliché, something that evoked perseverance — the resilience to never surrender in the face of adversity and the determination to forge an enduring musical body of work capable of standing the test of time."
— Danilo Bredda, in press interviews
2020

The Solitary Genesis & 25 Tracks on the Loop Station

Persevera's story began far from the spotlight through pure passion for Heavy Metal. Guitarist Danilo Bredda spent years crafting riffs, melodies and full arrangements using only his guitar connected to loop station pedals. Upon completing the 25th instrumental track, he decided this body of work needed a professional studio recording.

25 Songs Created • Seed of 2 Albums
2021

The Gathering & The Band Family

To give voice and substance to the songs, Danilo invited his cousin Paulo Carrara to take over lead vocals. Paulo embraced the project and introduced two foundational pillars: bassist Thadeu Castro ("Tio"), an improvisational maestro, and drummer Claudio Lima ("Radioativo"), whose studio in Sumaré became the band's creative and recording headquarters.

Private Studio • Organic & Independent Production
2023

Concept Album 'Genesis' & The Apocalypse Saga

The 25 original tracks were divided into two complete albums. The debut, Genesis (13 tracks), was released track by track with cinematic music videos on YouTube. The album captivated fans and critics with the Four Horsemen tetralogy (The White Rider, The Red Horse, Famine, Death) culminating in The Last Horseman. Its success sparked a partnership with MS Metal Agency Brasil for physical CD distribution nationwide.

Physical CD Release • MS Metal Agency Partnership
2024

The Triple Guitar Attack: Arrival of Zilds & Jésus

To push their sound to new heights of heaviness and melodic complexity, Persevera enlisted two accomplished guitarists and longtime friends: Leandro Pieroni ("Zilds") and Luiz Fernando Jésus ("Gordera"). Adopting the rare three-guitar frontline (reminiscent of Iron Maiden and Helloween), the music gained three-part harmonies, intertwined leads and a dense sonic wall.

3-Guitar Lineup • Triple Harmonized Solos
2026+

New Frontman, Sophomore Album & Stage Onslaught

In September 2026, Persevera inaugurated a fierce new chapter with the arrival of powerful lead vocalist Luis Carlos, taking over the helm following Paulo Carrara's memorable tenure on the Genesis debut. The band is currently tracking their 12-song sophomore album in studio — featuring blistering tracks like War is Hell, Master, The Chosen Son, Broken World and To the Unknown —, preparing an unstoppable live tour fueled by their three-guitar attack.

New Frontman Luis Carlos • Sophomore Album in Studio
